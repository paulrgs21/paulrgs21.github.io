I am a first-year PhD student in machine learning at Inria in Rennes, supervised by Titouan Vayer (Inria) and Franck Picard (CNRS, ENS de Lyon). My thesis, *Distributional Perspective of Self-Supervised Learning*, studies self-supervised methods through the distributions of the representations they learn.

Before my PhD, I studied at ENS Paris-Saclay, where I completed the MVA master's program (Mathematics, Vision, Learning), after a first year of master's in mathematics and statistics at Université Paris Dauphine-PSL. During my master's internship at Inria (2026), I studied dimensional collapse in self-supervised learning from a distributional point of view.

Research interests
------
* Self-supervised learning
* Representation learning
* Neural collapse
